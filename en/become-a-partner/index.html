The steps

  1. 1

    Online application

    You complete the application form.

  2. 2

    Automated confirmation

    You immediately receive a confirmation email.

  3. 3

    Required documentation

    You gather the documents requested for your file.

  4. 4

    Complete submission

    You send in all the documents.

  5. 5

    Review and onboarding

    The MC3 Cloud accounting team reviews your file and starts your onboarding.

  6. 6

    Extra details if needed

    Additional information may be requested from you.

Who is it for?

Resellers, MSPs, integrators and IT companies that sell cloud solutions to their own customers. MC3 Cloud does not sell to end customers.

What you get

  • Dedicated sales, technical and logistics resources based in several countries
  • Enhanced support and assistance
  • Partner and end-customer events, and webinars
  • A newsletter with news and updates
  • A marketplace onboarding session for new partners
