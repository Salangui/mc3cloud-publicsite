Cloud security

Configure Microsoft 365 security and strengthen protection with complementary solutions.

Security is often the best entry point to move a customer up the stack. We help you configure it and complete it.

Microsoft 365 security pack

Setup of Microsoft 365 security features: Defender for Office 365 and Defender for Business, device management with Intune, compliance with Microsoft Entra ID. Scope is tailored to the size of the business and its needs.

Complementary solutions

Solutions from specialist vendors, such as BullWall to contain ransomware or Acronis for endpoint and data protection, that you can pair with your projects.

Training

Cybersecurity and cloud training sessions, for your teams or for your customers.

What it covers

  • Microsoft 365 security pack: Defender for Office 365 and Defender for Business, Intune device management, Entra ID compliance
  • Complementary solutions: ransomware protection, endpoint and data protection
  • Cybersecurity and cloud training
