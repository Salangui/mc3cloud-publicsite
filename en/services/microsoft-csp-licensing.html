Microsoft CSP licensing

Microsoft Cloud licences and subscriptions, managed end to end from the partner marketplace.

MC3 Cloud is a Tier-2 Microsoft Cloud Solution Provider (CSP): we supply licences to resellers, who offer them to their end customers.

From the marketplace

  • Create tenants and provision subscriptions in a single action.
  • Add or remove licences immediately or at the renewal date.
  • Switch auto-renewal on or off and upgrade a subscription.
  • Manage customer tenant users and licences without going through the Microsoft 365 admin centre.
  • Follow billing and renewals and view statistics.

Getting started

Microsoft requires working with approved resellers: your company must be registered with Microsoft and have a Partner ID. New partners get a marketplace onboarding session.

What it covers

  • Microsoft 365, Azure, Dynamics 365 and Power Platform subscriptions
  • Tenant creation and provisioning in a single action
  • Add or remove licences, align end dates, manage renewals
  • Billing, indicators and sales statistics
