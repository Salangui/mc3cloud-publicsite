Identity and Endpoint Management

Expand your deployment capabilities with Microsoft Entra ID and Intune: secure access, PC and mobile device management, device provisioning and knowledge transfer.

Managing devices individually, maintaining inconsistent configurations and leaving personal device use unchecked all make IT management and data protection harder. MC3 Cloud works with your team to establish centralized management using Microsoft Entra ID and Microsoft Intune.

We support both initial deployments and the modernization of existing environments, within a scope agreed with you.

Design and deploy the environment

We assess existing identities, devices, applications and management practices to define the target configuration and plan the rollout.

Depending on the project, our teams can deliver:

  • Identity and access configuration: Microsoft Entra ID setup, multifactor authentication and Conditional Access policies.
  • PC and mobile device management: Intune enrollment, configuration settings, compliance policies and application deployment.
  • Windows device provisioning: Windows Autopilot configuration and the profiles needed to get devices ready for use.
  • BYOD controls: access rules for personal devices and data protection within supported applications.

Deployment can follow a phased approach, with validation through a pilot group before extending the rollout to the devices in scope.

Prepare your team for handover

The engagement includes configuration documentation and knowledge transfer. We work with your team to prepare procedures for employee onboarding, offboarding and device changes, without taking on their day-to-day execution.

The engagement ends with deployment and handover. Ongoing operations can be covered separately through Managed Services.

Example scenario

An MSP wants to standardize device management for a business whose employees work both in the office and remotely. MC3 Cloud configures Intune, access policies and applications, sets up Autopilot profiles and supports device enrollment before handing over the configurations and procedures to the MSP.

What it covers

  • Environment assessment and target configuration design
  • Identity, MFA and Conditional Access configuration
  • Intune deployment, enrollment and Windows Autopilot setup
  • Application, compliance and BYOD policy configuration
  • Documentation and knowledge transfer
