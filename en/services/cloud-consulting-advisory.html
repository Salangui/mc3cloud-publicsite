Azure infrastructure and projects

From architecture decisions to go-live, our teams help you deploy or migrate your customers’ environments on Microsoft Azure.

Turn customer requirements into a defined architecture

Does your customer need to replace ageing servers, move a business application to the cloud or establish a disaster recovery solution? We help you determine how Microsoft Azure can meet those needs, taking the existing environment, technical constraints and operational requirements into account.

During presales, we work with your team to choose an approach, define the scope and identify prerequisites. This sets the foundation for the deployment engagement: what will be delivered, who will handle each task and how the environment will go live.

Build, migrate and connect

Our teams can handle the technical delivery or work alongside yours, depending on the skills available and the project requirements.

Projects can include:

  • Infrastructure and applications: deploying virtual machines, migrating Hyper-V or VMware environments, hosting business applications and migrating SQL databases.
  • Azure foundations and hybrid environments: landing zones, networking, VPN connections and integration with existing Active Directory environments.
  • Business continuity: implementing backups with Azure Backup and preparing for disaster recovery with Azure Site Recovery.
  • Remote desktops and applications: deploying Azure Virtual Desktop or modernising an existing remote desktop environment.

Security, governance and consumption control are considered throughout the design and configuration of the environment.

Prepare your team for handover

At the end of the project, we provide knowledge transfer covering the deployed environment and the tasks needed to operate it. The content is tailored to your team’s experience and the responsibilities they will take on.

If you want us to handle some or all of the ongoing operations, this is covered by our managed services under a separate scope.

What it covers

  • Technical scoping and Azure architecture design
  • Azure foundation deployment and hybrid connectivity
  • Server, application and database migration
  • Backup, disaster recovery and virtual desktop deployment
  • Knowledge transfer for operational handover
