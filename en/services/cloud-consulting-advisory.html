Cloud consulting and scoping

A clear scope before you commit: needs, boundaries and proposal built with you and your customer.

A successful cloud project starts with a good scope. Our technical team works with you, and with your customer if you wish, to clarify the need before pricing it.

How it works

  1. Initial consultation: you contact your account manager or our technical team.
  2. Needs assessment: we gather the customer’s objectives and constraints with you.
  3. Planning and proposal: we coordinate scoping between you, our technical team and the customer, then formalise scope, deliverables and timeline.

What it covers

  • Initial consultation with your account manager or the technical team
  • Needs and objectives assessment
  • Engagement planning and proposal: scope, deliverables, timeline
