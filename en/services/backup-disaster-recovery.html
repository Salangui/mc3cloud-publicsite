Backup and disaster recovery

SaaS backup for Microsoft 365 and disaster recovery on Azure: two packs to protect your customers’ data.

Many customers who moved to the cloud do not think about backup. Yet vendors guarantee service availability, not data protection: that responsibility stays with the customer.

SaaS backup

Configuration of SaaS workload backup with Keepit (Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Microsoft Entra ID) or Acronis Cyber Protect (Microsoft 365 backup, Standard and Advanced editions).

Business continuity on Azure

Setup of disaster recovery with Azure Site Recovery, secure VPN configuration and Active Directory replication.

What it covers

  • SaaS backup configuration pack: Keepit (Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Entra ID) and Acronis Cyber Protect (Microsoft 365)
  • Azure business continuity pack: Azure Site Recovery, secure VPN, Active Directory replication
