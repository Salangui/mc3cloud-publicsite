A new website for our partners

Products by workload, services, support and online application: a tour of the new MC3 Cloud website.

NewsBy MC3 Cloud Team27 October 2026

Sample article: replace or delete it before going live.

The MC3 Cloud website has a new look. It is faster, available in English and French, and organised around one question: how can we help you sell more cloud to your customers?

What you will find

  • Products by workload: Microsoft 365, Azure, Dynamics 365, Power Platform, Security and Copilot, alongside solutions from Acronis, Keepit, BitTitan, Wondershare, Crossware and BullWall.
  • Services: migration, security, backup, training, pre-sales, Copilot adoption.
  • A Support & contact page that separates technical support from commercial requests.
  • Covered territories, grouped by major region.
