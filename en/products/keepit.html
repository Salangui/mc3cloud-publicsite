Keepit — SaaS Data Backup and Recovery

Offer independent, immutable SaaS backup for your customers’ Microsoft 365 and business applications, with granular recovery and no backup infrastructure to manage.

Protect SaaS data beyond the production environment

Keepit backs up your customers’ SaaS data in its own cloud, independently of the platforms it protects. Backup copies are immutable: they are designed to resist tampering and enable data recovery after accidental deletion, malicious activity or a ransomware attack.

For resellers, MSPs and integrators, this approach provides a consistent way to protect email, collaboration, identity and business application data through a unified platform.

Address real-world data loss scenarios

Keepit helps organizations:

  • Recover accidentally deleted items: find an email, document or record in their backups.
  • Prepare for ransomware recovery: maintain immutable copies separate from the production environment.
  • Respond to malicious deletion: search for and recover previously backed-up data.
  • Retain independent access to backed-up data when the SaaS service experiences an outage.
  • Plan data retention around business requirements and the terms of the selected offering.

One platform, a growing application portfolio

Keepit regularly expands its supported services. Its portfolio includes:

  • Microsoft 365: email, files, sites and collaboration data.
  • Microsoft Entra ID: supported identity objects and access configurations.
  • Microsoft Power Platform: Power Apps applications, Power Automate flows, Power Pages and Power BI content.
  • Microsoft Dynamics 365: data from supported applications, including Sales, Customer Service, Field Service, Project Service Automation and Marketing.
  • Microsoft Azure DevOps: Azure Boards, Azure Pipelines and Azure Repos.
  • Salesforce: standard and custom objects, files and attachments.
  • Google Workspace: Gmail, Google Drive, calendars, tasks, shared drives and Google Forms data.
  • Zendesk: tickets, users, organizations and customer service configurations.
  • Confluence Cloud: spaces, pages, attachments and related content.
  • Jira: projects, issues, configurations and workflows.
  • Jira Service Management: request, project and asset data.
  • Okta: identity objects, groups, applications and access policies.
  • Docusign: documents, envelopes, templates and related data.
  • BambooHR: employee data, files and HR information.
  • Miro: boards, spaces and collaborative content.
  • GitHub: supported Git repositories, configurations and collaboration data.
  • monday.com: boards, items, files and workspaces.
  • Autodesk Forma: project data, users, companies and workspaces.

Detailed protection for Microsoft 365

Keepit documents coverage across the main Microsoft 365 environments:

  • Exchange Online: Outlook email, contacts, tasks, calendars, in-place archives, public folders and subfolders, and user permissions.
  • OneDrive: documents, form templates, style libraries and versioning. Selection of the data to protect can use Microsoft Entra groups.
  • SharePoint: site collections, lists, document libraries, content types, permissions, customizations, pages and subsites.
  • Teams and Groups: conversations, calendars, Planner, files, channels, wiki pages, channel posts and replies, public and private channel files, tabs and associated SharePoint content.

Find the data and choose the right recovery method

Search helps administrators locate backed-up items before selecting the appropriate recovery method:

  • Granular restore of an email, file or record.
  • Bulk recovery to restore a larger dataset.
  • In-place restore, preserving supported structures and metadata.
  • Snapshot-based recovery to retrieve an earlier state.
  • Download, including PST export for Microsoft 365 email.
  • Secure sharing of recovered data through a link.

A 100% cloud-based service with no on-premises backup infrastructure

Keepit manages the backup infrastructure, including its operation, maintenance and updates. Customers do not need to install backup hardware.

The service includes unlimited hot storage, with no additional data ingress, egress, restore or transaction fees. This makes protection costs easier to assess, including when large-scale recovery is required.

Support compliance and data governance

Immutability, data retention, access controls and recovery capabilities can support customers’ compliance initiatives. Keepit highlights use cases related to GDPR, NIS2, HIPAA/HITRUST, FINRA and FISMA.
