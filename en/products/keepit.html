Keepit

SaaS backup in an independent cloud: Microsoft 365, Entra ID, Google Workspace and more.

Keepit backs up your customers’ SaaS data in an independent cloud built specifically for that purpose.

Why back up SaaS data

SaaS vendors guarantee the availability of their service, but protecting and backing up the data remains the customer’s responsibility. An accidental deletion, ransomware or an outage can be enough to lose critical data.

What you can offer

  • Backup of Microsoft 365 (email, OneDrive, SharePoint, Teams), Microsoft Entra ID, Google Workspace, Dynamics 365 and Power Platform.
  • Fast recovery thanks to smart search.
  • No hardware or software to install: storage is included.

With MC3 Cloud

  • Keepit available in our catalogue.
  • A service pack to configure SaaS backup.
