Wondershare — PDFs, Diagrams, Mind Maps and Video Creation

Offer PDFelement, EdrawMax, EdrawMind and Filmora to help customers work with PDFs, visualise processes, organise ideas and create videos with AI-powered tools.

Extend your offering with productivity and creative tools

Editing a PDF, explaining a process, preparing a workshop or producing a video: these needs span administrative teams, business managers, IT departments and marketing teams.

With PDFelement, EdrawMax, EdrawMind and Filmora, you can position Wondershare solutions around each customer's workflows. These four applications address complementary needs: document processing, diagramming, organising ideas and video production.

PDFelement: go beyond simply viewing PDFs

PDFelement brings together PDF creation, editing and conversion tools for teams that regularly work with contracts, invoices, reports or administrative documents.

Use cases include:

  • Editing and reusing documents: correct text, adjust images and convert PDFs into office document formats.
  • Working with scanned documents: use optical character recognition, or OCR, to make text searchable and editable.
  • Supporting document reviews: annotate files and use fillable forms.
  • Protecting information: apply restrictions and use redaction to remove sensitive data.
  • Handling repetitive tasks: use batch processing for recurring document operations.

Example use case: an administrative team needs to turn scanned records into usable documents, edit them and send them for review.

PDFelement also provides signing capabilities. Electronic signature availability depends on the edition, plan, region and purchasing channel, so this should be checked for the selected offering.

EdrawMax: make processes and projects easier to understand

EdrawMax is designed for professional diagramming: mapping a process, documenting an organisation or explaining a project to technical and business stakeholders. The vendor lists 280+ diagram types.

The solution allows users to:

  • create visual representations to document how an activity works;
  • import and export Microsoft Visio files;
  • import Visio shapes and stencils;
  • share files and invite others to comment or edit.

Example use case: an integrator prepares a diagram to explain a solution to a customer, then passes it on to the project delivery team.

For customers with existing Visio files, testing their own documents helps establish whether file exchange meets their requirements.

EdrawMind: turn ideas into a usable structure

EdrawMind, formerly MindMaster, helps organise information into mind maps and structured outlines. It combines visual thinking with note-taking to prepare meetings, organise knowledge or scope a project.

Use cases include:

  • Preparing a workshop: group ideas and organise the topics to cover.
  • Structuring a project: break an objective down into themes and actions.
  • Keeping context: attach detailed notes to topics within the map.
  • Presenting a proposal: navigate the mind map directly in presentation mode.

Example use case: a consultant organises requirements gathered during a customer workshop, then uses the map to present priorities and next steps.

Filmora: create and edit videos with AI

Filmora extends Wondershare's productivity offering with video editing software that includes artificial intelligence features. You can position it for marketing, communications and training teams preparing product presentations, tutorials or social media content.

Its capabilities include:

  • Generating video sequences: create content from text or animate images for use in a video project.
  • Simplifying editing: edit a video through its transcript, identify key moments and turn longer videos into short clips.
  • Refining footage: remove unwanted objects, isolate a subject and replace a background.
  • Creating and adapting images: generate visuals from a description, then adjust brightness, contrast or saturation and add them to the video project. The image generator described by the vendor is available on desktop.
  • Enhancing soundtracks: generate music and sound effects, reduce background noise and create voiceovers from text.
  • Adding subtitles: transcribe audio and create subtitles to accompany published content.

Example use case: a marketing team prepares a video introducing an offering, creates supporting visuals, removes a distracting object from a shot and adds music and subtitles before publishing.

Before preparing a quote, check the AI features included in the selected offering and the commercial usage rights for media assets. Generated images are subject to Filmora's terms of use.

Which solution should you recommend?

  • The customer mainly works with PDF documents: focus on PDFelement.
  • They need to map processes or create diagrams: recommend EdrawMax.
  • They want to organise ideas, notes or workshops: introduce EdrawMind.
  • They need to edit videos and create content with AI: propose Filmora.

These recommendations provide a framework for qualifying customer requirements. They do not imply that all four applications must be purchased together or are included in a single subscription.
