Microsoft Azure

Offer your customers cloud infrastructure tailored to their projects: servers, applications, backup and data, with Microsoft Azure and support from MC3 Cloud.

A cloud platform for your customers’ projects

Whether your customer needs to refresh their infrastructure, host a business application, prepare for disaster recovery or develop new data use cases, Microsoft Azure provides cloud services to address these needs.

The platform brings together compute, storage and networking resources, alongside database, analytics and artificial intelligence services. You can build a cloud environment or connect it to your customer’s existing infrastructure.

Practical use cases you can offer

  • Server hosting and migration: move virtual machines and business applications to Azure, or build new environments.
  • Backup and disaster recovery: protect data with Azure Backup and prepare environments for recovery with Azure Site Recovery.
  • Remote desktops and applications: provide an Azure Virtual Desktop environment tailored to users’ needs.
  • Hybrid infrastructure: connect Azure resources to the existing IT environment.
  • Applications, data and AI: use Azure services to host applications, work with data and develop new use cases.

From scoping to go-live with MC3 Cloud

MC3 Cloud enables you to offer Azure through the CSP programme, with consumption-based billing and usage tracking. You also have access to pre-sales support to assess your customer’s needs, define the technical approach and identify project prerequisites.

Depending on the agreed scope, our teams can handle the deployment or work alongside yours: configuring the Azure environment, establishing hybrid connectivity, migrating servers and databases, implementing backups or preparing for disaster recovery.

Security, governance and consumption control are considered in the environment’s design. At the end of the project, knowledge transfer prepares your team to take over the operations they will be responsible for.

A defined scope for each project

Azure resources and professional services address distinct needs. Project scoping defines the activities, responsibilities and go-live requirements. Any ongoing operations are covered by a separate managed services scope.

Commercial availability depends on the territory and applicable Microsoft rules. Contact our team to confirm the terms for your customer’s country.
