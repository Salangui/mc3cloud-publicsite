Microsoft Azure

Microsoft’s public cloud: compute, storage, networking, data and AI, billed on consumption.

Azure is Microsoft’s public cloud platform: virtual machines, storage, networking, databases and AI services. Consumption is billed to the reseller, then to their customer.

What you can offer

  • Migration of servers and virtual machines to Azure.
  • Backup and disaster recovery with Azure Backup and Azure Site Recovery.
  • New application and data projects on Azure.

With MC3 Cloud

  • Azure through the CSP programme, with consumption tracking.
  • Service packs to get started: migration to Azure and business continuity.
  • Specialised Azure pre-sales support.

Good to know

Microsoft applies different sales rules by territory: some Azure offers are not available everywhere. Contact us to check what applies in your country.
