Microsoft Security

Extend Microsoft 365 Business Premium with Defender Suite and Purview Suite, then expand protection with Microsoft Defender XDR, Sentinel and Security Copilot.

Build a security offering around Microsoft 365 Business Premium

For resellers, MSPs and integrators, Microsoft 365 Business Premium provides a starting point for securing Microsoft 365 environments in small and medium-sized businesses with up to 300 users. It combines productivity tools with protection for identities, devices, email and data.

The approach: make full use of the protection already available, identify additional requirements, then select add-ons that match each customer's risks and working practices.

Entra, Intune and Defender: protection already included

Business Premium brings together several complementary components:

  • Microsoft Entra ID P1: identity and access management to protect user accounts.
  • Microsoft Intune Plan 1: device and mobile application management, configuration and security policy enforcement.
  • Microsoft Defender for Business: endpoint protection against threats, including malware and ransomware.
  • Microsoft Defender for Office 365 Plan 1: protection for email and files against threats.
  • Microsoft Purview Information Protection and DLP for email and files: foundational capabilities to protect sensitive information and help prevent data loss.

These components provide a foundation that needs to be configured and adapted to each environment. They should not be confused with the additional capabilities available through Defender Suite and Purview Suite.

Microsoft Defender Suite: strengthen Business Premium against advanced threats

Microsoft Defender Suite for Microsoft 365 Business Premium is an add-on that extends Business Premium protection. It enables more comprehensive security across identities, endpoints, email and cloud applications.

It includes:

  • Microsoft Entra ID P2: advanced identity protection and access controls beyond the P1 level.
  • Microsoft Defender for Endpoint Plan 2: advanced endpoint investigation and response, threat hunting, remote intervention through Live Response and six months of device data retention.
  • Microsoft Defender for Office 365 Plan 2: automated investigation and response, attack simulation training and advanced threat analysis tools.
  • Microsoft Defender for Identity: detection and investigation of identity-related threats.
  • Microsoft Defender for Cloud Apps: visibility into SaaS applications, shadow IT discovery and controls for cloud application use.

For your customers, this add-on provides a path beyond foundational protection and better equips the teams responsible for detecting, understanding and responding to attacks.

Microsoft Purview Suite: protect data and strengthen compliance

Microsoft Purview Suite for Microsoft 365 Business Premium extends Business Premium with advanced data security, governance and compliance capabilities for organisations with up to 300 users.

It supports several areas:

  • Sensitive data discovery and classification: identifying, automatically labelling and protecting information.
  • Data loss prevention: DLP policies covering applications, devices and cloud services.
  • Insider risks and communications: Insider Risk Management and Communication Compliance to detect and review risky activity.
  • Investigations and traceability: eDiscovery Premium and Audit Premium.
  • Information lifecycle management: retention, deletion and records management.
  • Compliance tracking: Compliance Manager to assess posture and organise actions.
  • Additional controls: message encryption, Customer Lockbox, Customer Key, Information Barriers and Privileged Access Management.

With Audit Premium, Microsoft Entra ID, Exchange, OneDrive and SharePoint audit records are retained for one year by default. Other activities are retained for 180 days, unless a different applicable policy is configured. 10-year retention requires an additional add-on.

Purview Suite also supports preparation for AI adoption: identifying sensitive data, reducing overexposure and establishing controls for its use across Microsoft 365 and Copilot experiences. These tools support a compliance programme; they do not certify an organisation's compliance.

Defender Suite and Purview Suite address two complementary needs: protection against attacks and protection of information. Microsoft also offers a bundle combining both suites for Business Premium.

Microsoft Defender XDR and Microsoft Sentinel: connect security signals

Microsoft Defender XDR correlates signals and alerts from identities, endpoints, applications and email. The aim is to understand an incident as a whole rather than handle each alert in isolation. The components in Defender Suite contribute to this extended detection and response approach.

Microsoft Sentinel broadens visibility through its SIEM and SOAR capabilities: event collection and analysis, integration of Microsoft and third-party sources, detection, investigation and response automation. Its integration with Defender XDR brings this information together in a consistent security operations environment.

Sentinel requires separate capacity planning and Azure billing, including charges when billable data is ingested.

Microsoft Security Copilot: assist security teams with AI

Microsoft Security Copilot provides natural-language assistance for security professionals. It can help teams:

  • summarise incidents and put alerts into context;
  • prepare KQL queries for investigations;
  • analyse suspicious scripts;
  • understand risks and security policies;
  • prepare reports tailored to their audience.

It integrates with Microsoft Defender XDR, Microsoft Sentinel, Microsoft Intune and Microsoft Entra, among other products. Outputs should be reviewed by the teams conducting investigations and deciding which actions to take.
