Dynamics 365

Offer Microsoft CRM and ERP applications to connect sales, customer service, finance and operations, with CSP subscriptions through MC3 Cloud.

Business applications that connect teams and operations

Dynamics 365 brings together Microsoft applications for customer relationship management — CRM — and enterprise resource planning — ERP. They cover sales, customer service, finance and operations. Start with a specific business need, then extend the solution to other areas of the organisation.

For resellers and integrators, the starting point is the customer’s business processes: improving sales opportunity tracking, centralising business management, organising customer service or coordinating field operations.

Identify the right applications for each project

  • Dynamics 365 Business Central: bring SMB business management together. Connect finance, purchasing, sales and inventory to give teams a more consistent view of the business. Connections with Outlook, Excel and Teams bring business management closer to everyday working tools.
  • Dynamics 365 Sales: structure the sales process. Centralise accounts and contacts, track leads and opportunities, and support salespeople through each stage of the sales cycle.
  • Dynamics 365 Customer Service: organise customer enquiries and support cases. Track cases through to resolution, route them to the right people and give teams access to a knowledge base.
  • Dynamics 365 Field Service: coordinate field operations. Schedule service visits, assign resources and give technicians mobile access to work orders and equipment history.

The portfolio also includes Dynamics 365 Finance for financial management and Dynamics 365 Supply Chain Management for managing supply chain operations.

These applications address different needs: Dynamics 365 is not a single subscription that provides access to the entire portfolio. Each application is priced separately, with multiple plans available for some applications.

Extend Microsoft 365 and Power Platform use cases

Dynamics 365 can bring business data closer to Microsoft 365 collaboration tools. Power Platform allows you to extend the applications with automated processes and apps tailored to the customer’s needs.

This connection opens up conversations beyond office productivity: how can the customer share sales information, reduce duplicate data entry or adapt an approval workflow to the way their organisation works?

Apply AI to a specific business need

Copilot and agents extend the capabilities of Dynamics 365 applications. Documented use cases include summarising a case in Customer Service or a work order in Field Service.

For each project, first identify the task to improve, then check the available features, prerequisites and usage rights associated with the selected offering. AI should support a defined use case rather than serve as a broad promise.
