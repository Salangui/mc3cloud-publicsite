Acronis — Backup, Cybersecurity and IT Management

Build your backup, cybersecurity and IT management services with Acronis: protect workstations, servers and SaaS data through a multitenant platform.

Protect data and prepare for recovery through a shared platform

Backup enables data recovery. Endpoint protection helps counter attacks. Disaster recovery prepares systems to resume operation when the primary environment becomes unavailable.

Acronis Cyber Platform, formerly Acronis Cyber Protect Cloud, brings these capabilities together with IT management in a multitenant platform. Manage multiple customers through a shared console and select services that suit their environments, rather than adding separate tools for every requirement.

The capabilities described below span different Acronis services. Availability depends on licensing, enabled modules and supported environments: they are not all automatically included in a single offering.

Back up physical, virtual and cloud environments

Offer protection that matches your customers’ data and systems: files, system images, workstations, servers and virtual machines.

Depending on the selected solution, backups can use local or cloud storage. File recovery addresses accidental deletion, while full-system recovery helps respond to a failure or a more significant incident.

Immutable backup capabilities can provide additional protection against the deletion or alteration of recovery copies, depending on the chosen configuration.

The goal for your customer: recoverable data and a restoration process prepared before an incident occurs.

Protect Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace

SaaS data requires a protection strategy that addresses human error, deletion and security incidents. Acronis provides backup services for Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace.

For Microsoft 365, backup to Acronis Cloud covers:

  • Exchange Online: emails, calendars and contacts.
  • OneDrive for Business: files and folders.
  • SharePoint Online: supported sites and content.
  • Microsoft Teams: supported teams, channels and content.

Granular recovery lets you restore an individual item without recovering an entire mailbox or site. Supported Microsoft 365 backups can run up to six times per day.

Teams coverage has specific limitations: private one-to-one and group chats are not backed up. Channel conversations can be downloaded as HTML files but cannot be restored into Teams.

Bring prevention, detection and recovery together

Build protection that goes beyond backup. Depending on the enabled services, Acronis solutions can combine:

  • Anti-malware and anti-ransomware protection.
  • Endpoint detection and response with EDR, and XDR integration.
  • Email and collaboration security.
  • Data loss prevention with DLP.
  • Backup and recovery to complement incident response.

For partners, the value lies in connecting protection, investigation and recovery activities, without positioning backup as a replacement for cybersecurity.

Prepare and test disaster recovery

Backup preserves data; disaster recovery prepares systems to operate in a recovery environment.

With Acronis Disaster Recovery services, you can prepare failover scenarios and test recovery servers in a network isolated from production. These tests help verify interactions between multiple systems before a real incident occurs.

Each project should define priority applications, dependencies, connectivity and recovery objectives. Expected recovery times must be assessed and tested for the specific environment rather than presented as a general guarantee.

Centralise your customers’ IT operations

Acronis RMM complements protection with administration and monitoring capabilities:

  • Hardware and software inventory.
  • Vulnerability assessment.
  • Patch management for Microsoft products and 300+ third-party applications.
  • Monitoring of 24 system and hardware metrics for Windows and macOS, alongside custom metrics.
  • Task automation through scripts.
  • Remote access and user assistance.

PSA capabilities complement these tools with IT service management. This helps bring technical operations, service tracking and business management processes closer together.

Build recurring services around customer needs

Rather than offering every module by default, start with the requirement you need to address:

  • Managed backup: protect data and organise recovery.
  • SaaS protection: back up Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace.
  • Business continuity: prepare and test a recovery environment.
  • Endpoint security: combine prevention, detection and response.
  • Endpoint management: monitor devices, apply patches and provide remote support.

These are potential approaches to building your own services, not predefined MC3 Cloud packages. Multitenant management and modular activation let you expand protection as each customer’s requirements evolve.

Move forward with MC3 Cloud

MC3 Cloud supports your Acronis backup and disaster recovery projects with:

  • Definition of the data and systems to protect.
  • Configuration of SaaS, server and workstation backups.
  • Preparation of disaster recovery arrangements.
  • Restore or failover testing and handover to your team.
  • Assistance during incidents, based on the recovery arrangements in place and the agreed scope.
