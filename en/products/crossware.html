Crossware Email Signature — Signatures, Campaigns and Legal Disclaimers

Centralise your customers’ email signatures, legal disclaimers and campaigns across Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Exchange and HCL Domino, with an option to deploy in their own Azure environment.

More than a signature at the end of an email

Crossware Email Signature brings professional signatures, legal disclaimers and marketing content together in a centrally managed platform. Organisations can maintain consistent communications while tailoring signatures to users, teams and recipients.

The solution supports Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Microsoft Exchange and HCL Domino. For resellers, MSPs and integrators, it complements email and collaboration projects by combining consistent branding with fewer manual updates.

Build modular signatures with conditional content

Create signatures from separate blocks for contact details, logos, banners, links and legal disclaimers. The designer provides drag-and-drop tools, customisable templates, and rich text and HTML editors.

Rules can apply to an entire signature or an individual block, based on:

  • the sender or recipient;
  • the team or department;
  • internal or external communication;
  • a new message or a reply;
  • a date range or campaign.

This approach keeps a common structure while adapting the relevant content. User-specific previews help check the result before it goes live.

Centralise management and delegate content editing

Signatures are administered through a central interface, without asking every employee to change their settings.

Selected users or groups can be given access to create and assign signatures without becoming full administrators. Marketing teams can manage the content entrusted to them, while IT retains administrative control.

Populate contact details automatically

Crossware uses directory information to populate names, job titles and contact details. Custom attributes can also include professional information specific to the organisation.

In Microsoft 365, custom fields can use attributes accessible through Microsoft Graph. Google Workspace uses its directory, Exchange draws on Active Directory, and HCL Domino uses information from its directory environment.

Turn everyday emails into a marketing channel

Add promotional banners, links and calls to action to everyday communications. Content can be tailored to teams, recipients or campaign periods.

Crossware also documents integrations for one-click surveys, customer feedback and review requests. Signatures become an additional channel supporting sales and marketing activities.

Keep legal disclaimers under central control

Legal disclaimers and corporate information are defined centrally and applied according to the selected rules. Maintaining them no longer depends on individual employees updating their signatures.

Centralised management helps apply wording approved by the organisation; it does not, on its own, guarantee regulatory compliance.

Apply signatures across desktop, web and mobile

For Microsoft 365, Crossware can add signatures during message processing after the user sends an email. This covers emails sent from Outlook, the web and mobile devices without relying on a locally configured signature on each device.

Enterprise Private Cloud: process emails in the customer’s Azure environment

Crossware Enterprise Private Cloud allows the service to be deployed in the organisation’s own Microsoft Azure infrastructure.

Messages follow this path:

  1. The user sends an email through Microsoft 365.
  2. The message is routed to the Crossware service hosted in the customer’s Azure environment.
  3. Crossware applies the signature.
  4. The message returns to Microsoft 365 for delivery to the recipient.

This model is worth considering when control over the processing environment is a selection criterion, including regulated organisations, sovereignty requirements or internal hosting policies. Its suitability must be assessed against the customer’s specific requirements rather than assuming automatic compliance.

Choose the right model for the email environment

  • Microsoft 365: cloud-based signature application.
  • Google Workspace: browser-based management and processing within the solution’s cloud infrastructure.
  • Microsoft Exchange: installation on the organisation’s Exchange server.
  • HCL Domino: installation on the organisation’s Domino server.
  • Enterprise Private Cloud: processing of Microsoft 365 messages within the customer’s Azure infrastructure.
