Microsoft 365

Offer your customers Microsoft 365 plans that match their needs: apps, email, collaboration and security, with support from MC3 Cloud.

Plans that match how your customers work

Business email, productivity apps, file sharing, collaboration and security: Microsoft 365 plans address a range of user needs. Selecting the right licenses depends on how people work, the number of employees and each organisation’s security requirements.

With MC3 Cloud, you benefit from pre-sales support to select the right plans and services to complement your customer projects.

For SMBs: Microsoft 365 Business

Business plans are designed for organisations with up to 300 users:

  • Microsoft 365 Business Basic: for employees who mainly work online, with web and mobile apps, business email and file sharing.
  • Microsoft 365 Business Standard: adds desktop apps, including Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook, so employees can also work offline.
  • Microsoft 365 Business Premium: brings together productivity tools and security capabilities to help protect users, devices and business data.

Business Premium: bringing productivity and security together

For SMBs looking beyond productivity apps alone, Business Premium provides a foundation for working and protecting their IT environment. In addition to Business Standard capabilities, the plan includes:

  • Access control with Microsoft Entra ID P1: apply Conditional Access policies to better control connections to business resources.
  • Device and app management with Microsoft Intune Plan 1: centralise configurations and security policies for computers and mobile devices.
  • Endpoint protection with Microsoft Defender for Business: strengthen security for the devices employees use.
  • Email and file protection with Microsoft Defender for Office 365 Plan 1: add protection for email and collaboration tools.
  • Information protection with Microsoft Purview: classify and protect sensitive data, and apply data loss prevention policies to emails and files.

For resellers and MSPs, this combination supports an offering that goes beyond productivity licenses, connecting everyday work with the customer’s IT management and security needs.

For organisations with more than 300 users: Microsoft 365 Enterprise

Microsoft 365 Enterprise plans address the needs of organisations looking to deploy workplace tools at scale and strengthen the management of their environment:

  • Microsoft 365 E3: a foundation for productivity, identity and device management, with desktop apps, collaboration services and Windows Enterprise.
  • Microsoft 365 E5: extends threat protection, identity security, data protection and compliance, with analytics capabilities through Power BI Pro.
  • Microsoft 365 E7: brings AI into business workflows while providing controls for its deployment. The plan combines Microsoft 365 E5, Microsoft 365 Copilot, Microsoft Entra Suite and Microsoft Agent 365 to bring together productivity, security and AI agent governance.

For frontline teams: Microsoft 365 Frontline

Employees working in stores, in the field or directly with customers do not necessarily have the same needs as office-based teams. Frontline plans give them access to information and tools that support their work:

  • Microsoft 365 F1: for viewing company information, accessing shared resources and organising tasks. Word, Excel and PowerPoint on the web and mobile are read-only; the plan does not include rights to use an Exchange mailbox.
  • Microsoft 365 F3: for teams that also need to create and edit documents using web and mobile apps, use Exchange Kiosk email and benefit from Windows Enterprise and device management with Intune Plan 1.

These plans can complement office-based employees’ licenses within the same organisation, subject to each plan’s eligibility requirements.

With MC3 Cloud: from licenses to customer projects

  • Select the right plans: work with our pre-sales teams to identify licenses that match your customers’ needs.
  • Manage subscriptions: use the MC3 Cloud marketplace to track licenses, renewals and billing, in accordance with subscription terms.

Complementary services for your projects

  • Microsoft Cost and License Optimization: review usage, identify unused licenses and overlapping tools, then align subscriptions with your customers’ needs.
  • Migration services: entrust us with email and file migrations to Microsoft 365, or between tenants, within the scope agreed with your team.
  • Microsoft 365 security assessment and hardening: identify configuration weaknesses and receive a prioritised action plan, with optional implementation of the recommended improvements.

Subscription terms and renewals

  • Term length: monthly or annual commitment, depending on the plan.
  • Renewal: subscriptions renew automatically by default to maintain service continuity, unless auto-renewal is disabled before the renewal date.
  • Cancellation or reduction: available within 7 days (168 hours) of purchase or renewal, with a prorated refund for the unused period.
  • Changes: add licenses or move to a higher-tier plan at any time, subject to Microsoft’s permitted upgrade paths, without a new cancellation window.
