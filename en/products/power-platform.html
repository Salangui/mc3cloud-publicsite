Microsoft Power Platform

Offer business apps, process automation and dashboards with Microsoft Power Platform, supported by MC3 Cloud services.

Turn your customers’ needs into business solutions

Requests tracked by email, information re-entered across several tools and reports rebuilt every month: these are practical starting points for a Power Platform project.

Microsoft’s tools support application development, process automation, data analysis and website creation. Their low-code approach uses visual tools to make solutions easier to build, while retaining extensibility options for developers.

For resellers, MSPs and integrators, the starting point is a specific business need: simplifying an approval, centralising request tracking or giving managers a clearer view of their operations.

Choose the right tools for each use case

  • Power Apps — build business applications. Replace fragmented tracking with an application tailored to the customer’s process: submitting a request, tracking its progress or organising tasks. Power Apps can work with Power Automate to connect the application to the actions required.
  • Power Automate — automate tasks and approvals. Organise approvals, notifications, reminders and information transfers between tools. Leave requests, purchase approvals and employee onboarding are all potential use cases.
  • Power BI — use data to monitor business performance. Bring sources together, build interactive reports and track the metrics teams need: sales, margins, budgets or operational activity.
  • Power Pages — build websites connected to business data. Extend internal applications with web experiences based on the organisation’s data and the access controls defined for the project.

These tools can be used independently or combined. Microsoft Dataverse provides a shared data foundation for applications and automation.

Connect applications to the tools customers already use

Power Platform can extend Microsoft 365, Dynamics 365 and Azure, as well as connect to other systems. Connectors integrate the data and actions needed for the chosen process.

Example scenario: an employee submits a purchase request in Power Apps. Power Automate sends it to the appropriate approvers, records their decision and notifies the requester. A Power BI report can then help analyse requests and how they are handled. This is an example of a solution to design, not a feature automatically delivered with the licences.

Plan access, governance and licensing from the outset

A project involves more than building an application or a flow. It also requires defining who will use it, which data they can access, which connections are allowed and how the solution will be maintained. Power Platform provides environment administration, security and governance capabilities to support these controls.

Licensing needs to be assessed against the selected components and scenarios. Microsoft 365 use rights do not cover every use case: access to premium, on-premises and custom connectors requires an appropriate standalone Power Apps or Power Automate licence.
